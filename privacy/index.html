Who we are

Conexión is operated by IndieLiber Studio LLC. Contact us at IndieLiber@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.

Conexión is an early Spanish conversation-practice app for adults. The characters and conversations are fictional. It checks a finite set of learned replies; other wording may also be valid Spanish. Sound feedback is experimental and does not measure accent quality, fluency, or romantic success.

Your practice stays on your phone

Your selected answers, custom place names or book titles, learning progress, help used, and practice history are saved locally on your phone. These personal answers are not included in usage reporting.

The app does not create public profiles or connect learners to one another. Local progress is separate from reporting consent and remains available if reporting fails or you decline it.

Microphone and audio

The microphone is used when you start speaking practice. Conexión uses supported on-device speech recognition and a bundled local sound model. If the required on-device support is unavailable, you can type your answer instead.

Recorded microphone audio is held in memory for the current attempt. Conexión does not save it as an audio file or upload it. The limited sound-history records do not contain a replayable recording.

Usage reporting is optional

You may choose to share usage data to help us find confusing practice questions and technical problems. You can decline and keep using the practice features. Reporting is off until you give permission.

When enabled in a connected build, Conexión sends explicitly defined events to PostHog. These cover studying a phrase, submitting an answer, using help, receiving a sound-review outcome, completing a round or pack, and rating feedback. The reporting design also supports an onboarding-completion event.

What an event can include

  • A random installation identifier and a session identifier.
  • The event time and app version.
  • Pack and card identifiers, input type, help category, and result category.
  • Numeric practice scores when available.

The random installation identifier links events from the same installation. Conexión does not associate it with your name or email.

What is not included

Events do not include recordings, recognized speech or typed answer text, personal-answer choices, custom names or book titles, pronunciation sound labels, contacts, or an advertising identifier. Conexión does not enable session replay, screenshots, automatic interaction capture, or person-profile enrichment.

Consented events go to PostHog’s US ingestion endpoint. Network delivery is needed for reporting; it is not needed to upload audio because Conexión does not upload audio. The app asks PostHog not to derive geolocation. That setting does not guarantee that ordinary network metadata is absent from the provider’s logs.

Changing your choice

You can turn reporting off in the app’s privacy and beta-feedback controls. This stops further reporting, cancels pending delivery, clears unsent events, and discards the local reporting identifier. Requests already transmitted may arrive while cancellation takes effect.

Opting out cannot recall events that have already reached the provider. If you enable reporting again, the app creates a new local reporting identifier.

While offline, consented events may wait locally for a connection. The queue holds no more than 500 events. Events older than seven days are removed when the reporting service next processes the queue. This is a limit on the local queue, not a promise about how long the provider retains events already received.

Practice remains available offline once the required device speech support is available. If reporting is not configured in your build, the app explains that and does not collect an upload queue.

Feedback, support, and deletion requests

The “Was this feedback helpful?” control reports only the chosen helpful or not-helpful category when reporting is enabled. Written feedback opens an email for you to review and send yourself to IndieLiber@gmail.com. No email is sent automatically.

Anything you choose to include in that email is shared with IndieLiber Studio LLC through your email provider. Please avoid including recordings or sensitive biographical details unless you deliberately want to share them.

For privacy questions or a deletion request, contact IndieLiber@gmail.com. Because Conexión does not link analytics identifiers to your email or name, we may be unable to locate your particular analytics events from an email address alone. Contact us for questions about information already received by us or the analytics provider; no immediate deletion or fixed remote-retention guarantee is made here.

Other services and this website

TestFlight and your email provider handle their own information and diagnostics. This notice does not claim to control Apple’s collection or the information you choose to send through email.

This privacy and support website does not embed analytics scripts or collect contact-form responses. Its hosting service processes web requests to serve these pages.

This notice applies to the Conexión beta described above. Any campaign, waitlist, or other service that collects additional information needs its own accurate disclosure.
