A few useful answers

Can I practice without the microphone?

Yes. Choose “Type instead” during a question round. Typed practice does not require microphone permission. Speaking needs supported on-device Spanish recognition; the app explains when that support is unavailable.

Why did the app not accept my Spanish?

The beta matches a finite set of authored replies and their supported alternatives. Other wording may still be valid Spanish. Try the reply you studied or use practice help. If the result was confusing, tell us which pack and question you were using.

Why did my grade stay the same after a retry?

The grade records each card’s original presentation. Later independent recall completes the card without changing that original grade. A new round gives you another chance to improve your grade.

Does sound feedback grade my accent?

No. Sound feedback is experimental and separate from conversation completion. It is not an accent, fluency, or intelligibility score. If feedback is confusing, tell us whether the recording was noisy or cut off and what part of the advice was unclear. You do not need to send a recording.

Do I have to share usage data?

No. Reporting is optional, and practice works if you decline. You can change your choice in the app’s privacy and beta-feedback controls. Read the privacy notice for the exact information reporting can include.

What if a personal answer does not fit me?

Choose a truthful authored option, use a supported custom place name or book title, or skip the question. Skipping does not count as mastering that phrase. You do not need to share personal details you would rather keep private.

Privacy requests

Email IndieLiber@gmail.com for a privacy question or deletion request. Please avoid sending sensitive biographical details. Analytics identifiers are not linked to your name or email, so we may not be able to locate particular analytics events from your email alone.
